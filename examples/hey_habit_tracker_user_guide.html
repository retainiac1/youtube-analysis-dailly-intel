Welcome

What is Hey Habit Tracker?

Hey Habit Tracker is your personal habit companion. It helps you build good habits and break bad ones through simple, visual tracking.

Whether you want to drink more water, exercise regularly, read more books, or cut back on coffee — this app makes it easy to see your progress and stay motivated.

What Makes It Different?

🎙️

Voice-First

On iPhone, log habits just by talking. Say “8 glasses of water” and you’re done.

✨

Simple & Beautiful

No complicated features. Just clear progress rings that show exactly how you’re doing.

🔒

Your Data Stays Yours

Everything is stored on your device. We don’t collect, sync, or share your habit data.

Who Is This For?

Hey Habit Tracker is perfect if you:

  • ✓ Want to build positive daily routines
  • ✓ Prefer visual progress over complicated spreadsheets
  • ✓ Value your privacy
  • ✓ Like the idea of hands-free voice logging (iPhone users)
  • ✓ Want something simple that actually works

Core Concepts

What is a “Habit” in This App?

A habit is anything you want to track regularly. It could be:

  • Something you do (exercise, meditation, reading)
  • Something you consume (water, coffee, calories)
  • Something you avoid (smoking, screen time, junk food)

Each habit has a target — the amount you’re aiming for each day, week, or month.

Build Habits vs. Break Habits

📈 Build Habits

Things you want to do MORE of. Your goal is to reach or exceed your target.

📉 Break Habits

Things you want to do LESS of. Your goal is to stay under your limit.

The app shows your progress differently for each type, so you always know if you’re on track.

Understanding Your Progress

Your progress is shown as a percentage:

  • 100% = You hit your target!
  • Over 100% = You exceeded your goal (great for build habits!)
  • Under 100% = You still have room to go
Example: If your water target is 8 glasses and you’ve had 6: Progress = 6 ÷ 8 = 75%. The progress ring fills to 75%.

Daily Tracking Flow

A typical day looks like this:

  1. 1
    Morning — Open the app and see yesterday’s results
  2. 2
    Throughout the day — Log entries as you go (voice or manual)
  3. 3
    Evening — Check your progress and see if you hit your targets
  4. 4
    Repeat — Build momentum day by day

No pressure to be perfect. Just show up and track honestly.

Creating Habits

Step-by-Step Guide

1
Open the habit creator

From the home screen, tap + in the Quick Actions area, or go to Settings → My Habits → Add New

2
Name your habit

Type something short and clear: “Water”, “Exercise”, “Reading”. The app will suggest an icon based on the name.

3
Choose your habit type

Build: Things you want to do more of. Break: Things you want to limit.

4
Set your target

Enter the number you’re aiming for. For build habits, this is your goal to reach. For break habits, this is your maximum limit.

5
Set a default value (optional)

A quick-log amount for one-tap logging. Example: Set “1” for water so each tap logs 1 glass.

6
Choose your unit

What are you measuring? Glasses, minutes, steps, pages, cups — type any unit that makes sense to you. Note: on the Home screen only the first 3 letters of the unit will be displayed.

7
Pick your timeframe

Daily: Resets every day at midnight. Weekly: Sunday to Saturday. Monthly: Calendar month.

8
Choose a color and icon

Pick a color that feels right and choose an emoji icon (or keep the suggested one).

9
Save!

Tap Save and your habit is ready to track.

Record Your Why (Optional)

After you save a habit, you can record a short voice memo explaining why it matters to you. When motivation dips, hearing your own reason in your own voice is a powerful nudge to keep going.

  1. 1Open a habit for editing and tap Record Your Why under the Your Why section
  2. 2Tap record and speak for up to 30 seconds — say why this habit is important to you
  3. 3Play it back, optionally transcribe it to text, then save. You can re-record or delete it anytime.
Remind Me Why: Turn this on for a habit and the app will surface your recording as a gentle nudge on the home screen when the habit needs attention.

Your recording and its transcription stay on your device — like the rest of your data, they are never uploaded.

Advanced Options

Custom Synonyms (for Siri)

Add alternate names so Siri recognizes a habit by what you naturally say. For example, add “espresso” or “latte” as synonyms for a habit named “Coffee.” You will find this in the Personalize section when creating or editing a habit.

Baseline (Break habits only)

For Break habits you can set an optional baseline — the starting amount you are reducing from — so the app can show how much you have cut back. Leave it blank and the app uses your highest recorded day as the baseline automatically.

Tracking Your Progress

Logging with Voice (iPhone Only)

This is the magic of Hey Habit Tracker on iPhone! Instead of typing, just talk.

How it works:

You don’t even need to have the app open —

  1. 1Just say “Hey Siri, log habit in Hey Habit Tracker”
  2. 2Siri will respond: “What would you like to log?”
  3. 3Then say the amount and habit name. For example: “10 grams of protein”
  4. 4Siri will confirm by saying “Logged 10 grams of protein”

That’s it — two steps, completely hands-free.

Other supported phrases to engage Siri:

  • “Hey Siri, track habit in Hey Habit Tracker”
  • “Hey Siri, record habit in Hey Habit Tracker”
  • “Hey Siri, add habit in Hey Habit Tracker”
Setup: Siri Shortcuts are registered automatically when you create habits. No extra setup needed. You can find more details in the app under Settings → Siri Help.

Tips for voice logging:

  • Speak clearly and naturally
  • If Siri doesn’t understand, it will ask you to repeat
  • You can always switch to manual entry (see below)

Manual Entry (All Platforms)

When you prefer typing:

  1. 1Tap the Log tab or tap the habit’s button under Quick Actions on the Home Page
  2. 2Select your habit from the dropdown if your navigation path was through Log
  3. 3Enter the amount
  4. 4Add a note if you want (optional)
  5. 5Adjust the date/time if needed
  6. 6Tap Save

Quick Actions from Home Screen

The fastest way to log habits:

  1. 1Look at the Quick Actions section on the home screen
  2. 2Each habit has its own button
  3. 3Tap once to log your default value

Example: If your Water habit has a default of 1, each tap logs 1 glass.

Set up default values when creating habits to make this super quick!

Concentric progress rings showing all your habits at once. Each ring fills based on your percentage toward the target.

⚡

Quick Actions

Buttons for your habits. Tap to log with your default value. The + button adds new habits.

Progress Tab

Tap the Progress tab for deeper insights.

Overview Mode: Summary cards showing Goals Met, Needs Work, and Average Progress, plus your progress visualization and actual vs. target numbers.

Time Periods:

  • Today — Just today’s progress
  • Week — Last 7 days
  • Month — This month so far
  • All — Your complete history

Charts

Tap “Charts” in the Progress tab to see trends:

Bar Chart

Daily totals as vertical bars with a target line. Scroll left to see past days.

Line Chart

Your progress as a flowing line. Great for spotting trends over time.

Heatmap

A calendar-style grid. Darker colors = better performance. Shows all habits at once.

Understanding the Heatmap: The heatmap uses color intensity to show your daily performance at a glance. Darker cells mean you were closer to (or exceeded) your target. Lighter cells mean you fell short. This makes it easy to spot patterns — your best days of the week, consistent stretches, and days you missed.

Managing Habits

Editing Existing Habits

Made a mistake or want to change your target?

  1. 1Go to Settings → My Habits
  2. 2Find the habit you want to change
  3. 3Tap the Edit button
  4. 4Make your changes
  5. 5Tap Save

You can change anything: name, target, icon, color, habit type.

Archiving vs. Deleting

Archive

Hides a habit but keeps all your history. Great for seasonal habits. You can restore it anytime.

Edit the habit → Tap “Archive” in the Danger Zone section → Confirm

Delete

Removes the habit completely. All history for that habit is gone. Use only if you’re sure.

To restore archived: Settings → My Habits → Archived Habits → Restore

Editing & Deleting Entries

Made a logging mistake? No problem.

To edit an entry: Go to the Data tab → Find the entry → Tap Edit → Change the amount, date, time, or notes → Tap Save

To delete an entry: Go to the Data tab → Find the entry → Tap Delete → Confirm deletion

Undo! After deleting, you have 5 seconds to tap “Undo” and get it back.

Tips for Success

1

Start Small

Research shows that more than 7 habits is too difficult for most people to track, so Hey Habit Tracker has a 7 habit limit. Track just 2–3 habits at first. Pick your top priorities and nail those first. You can always add more later.

2

Use Default Values

Set up default values for one-tap logging: Water = 1 glass, Exercise = 15 minutes, Reading = 10 pages. Makes logging so fast you’ll actually do it.

3

Don’t Chase Perfection

Missing a day doesn’t erase your progress. The app shows averages and trends, not just streaks. A few missed days in a great month is still a great month!

4

Review Weekly

Take 5 minutes each week to check the Week view, celebrate what went well, notice patterns, and adjust targets if needed.

5

Make It Visible

Keep the app on your phone’s home screen. The more you see it, the more you’ll use it.

6

Pair Habits with Triggers

Attach habits to things you already do: “After my morning coffee, I’ll log my water goal.” “Before bed, I’ll review my day.”

7

Be Honest

Log what actually happened, not what you wish happened. The data is for YOU. Honest tracking leads to real insights.

Troubleshooting

Voice Not Working?

Check microphone permission: Go to your phone’s Settings → Find Hey Habit Tracker → Make sure Microphone is enabled.

Check Siri is enabled: Go to your phone’s Settings → Siri → Talk to Siri → Enable Siri or Hey Siri.

Try these fixes:

  • Speak more clearly
  • Reduce background noise

Still not working? Use manual entry instead. Voice features require iOS Siri.

Data Not Saving?

Check if the session paused: The app pauses your session after 5 minutes of inactivity to save battery. You’ll see a paused overlay — just tap to resume.

Make sure you tapped Save: Entries aren’t saved until you confirm. Look for the Save button after entering data.

Force close and reopen: Swipe the app away completely, then open it again fresh.

App Running Slow?

Try these steps:

  1. Close other apps running in the background
  2. Restart your device
  3. Make sure you have enough storage space
  4. Delete old test data if you generated any

Privacy & Your Data

🔒 Your data stays on your device. Period.

Your habit data is stored locally on your phone, tablet, or computer. It never leaves your device unless YOU choose to export it.

Crash Reporting & Analytics

To improve app stability, we use Sentry for crash reporting. This collects:

  • Anonymous session data (app opens, crashes)
  • Error reports with device platform info
  • No habit data, names, or logged entries are ever sent

Emails and IP addresses are stripped before any data is transmitted.

What We Don’t Collect

  • ✗ We don’t track what habits you create
  • ✗ We don’t collect personal information (name, email, location)
  • ✗ We don’t have accounts or logins
  • ✗ We don’t sync your habit data to any cloud servers

Exporting Your Data

Want a backup or to analyze your data elsewhere?

JSON Export

Full backup. Contains everything: habits, entries, settings. Can be imported back.

CSV Export

Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers. Perfect for custom charts.

Database Export

Raw SQLite .db file. For advanced users or full backups.

How to export: Go to the Data tab → Tap the export button → Choose your format → Share or save the file.

Deleting Your Data

  • Delete individual entries: Data tab → Find entry → Delete
  • Delete a habit and its history: Settings → My Habits → Edit → Delete
  • Delete EVERYTHING: Settings → Clear All Data (requires confirmation, cannot be undone)

FAQ

General Questions

Is Hey Habit Tracker free?

Hey Habit Tracker offers a 3-day free trial so you can explore everything the app has to offer.

Do I need an account?

No account needed. Just download and start tracking.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Everything works without internet. Your data is stored locally.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

The app works on each device independently. There’s no automatic sync between devices, but you can export from one and import to another.

Habits & Tracking

How many habits can I track?

You can track up to 7 habits at a time. If you need to add more, archive an existing habit to make room.

Can I track the same habit multiple times a day?

Absolutely! Each log entry is separate. Log water in the morning, afternoon, and evening — they all add up.

What’s the difference between Daily, Weekly, and Monthly targets?

Daily targets reset at midnight. Weekly targets span Sunday through Saturday. Monthly targets span the calendar month. Weekly and monthly give you flexibility on which days you do what.

Can I backdate an entry?

Yes! When logging, you can change the date and time to any point in the past. You can change any field in the Log screen or the Data tab.

Voice Features

What is “Your Why”?

“Your Why” is an optional voice memo you can record for any habit, explaining why it matters to you. The app keeps it on your device and can play it back as a “Remind Me Why” nudge when you need motivation. Record it from the Your Why section when creating or editing a habit.

Data & Privacy

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Since data is stored locally, uninstalling removes everything. Export a backup first if you want to keep your history.

Can I transfer data to a new phone?

Export your data as .db, transfer the file to your new phone, then import it in the app.

Is my data encrypted?

Your data is stored in a local file on your device. Export files are not encrypted, so store them securely.

Troubleshooting

What do the heatmap colors mean?

Darker colors mean you were closer to your target that day. Lighter colors mean you fell short. Gaps in color mean no data was logged.

Why does the app show a paused screen?

The app pauses your session after 5 minutes of inactivity to save battery. Just tap to resume where you left off.

I logged something wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes! Go to the Data tab, find the entry, and tap Edit to change it or Delete to remove it.
